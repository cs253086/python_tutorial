## Next class → 🏗️ The `class` keyword

Right now your bird is just a circle. Real games have lots of
moving things (a bird, pipes, maybe clouds). We don't want to
copy-paste pen setup for every one — that's where **classes**
come in.

A `class` is like a **cookie cutter**: you write it once, then
stamp out as many cookies (birds) as you want, each with its own
position, color, and behavior.

In Class 2 you'll meet `class Bird:` for the first time and turn
the plain circle into a proper bird with an eye and a beak.

# 🏆 You did it!

You finished **Project 4 — Make a Face!** 😃

## What you built

- A reusable kit of four shape helpers: `circle`, `dot`, `rect`,
  and `triangle`
- A face (or monster, or alien) of your own design

## 🧠 What you used

- **Functions with parameters** (each shape helper takes
  `x, y, size, color`)
- **`turtle` drawing** with `begin_fill`, `forward`, `left`,
  `end_fill`
- **`for` loops** inside `rect` (4 sides) and `triangle` (3 sides)
- **Your own creativity!**

No new Python concepts — you used everything from Projects 1–3.
That's what Design projects are for: practice + creativity.
